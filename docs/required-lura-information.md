# Required Lura information before launch

Add only information approved by Lura. The public website keeps all catalog content unpublished until an administrator enters and publishes it.

- Official company introduction and homepage copy
- Current product catalog, categories, specifications, images, manuals, and warranty terms
- Verified installation project details and permission to publish client/location names and media
- Actual services and solution categories
- Official email, phone, WhatsApp, address, and social links
- Approved testimonials and FAQs
- Privacy notice and consent wording reviewed for the intended operating jurisdictions
- Production hosting, PostgreSQL provider, domain, TLS, backups, and email delivery configuration
- Administrator owner's operational contact and credential rotation procedure
